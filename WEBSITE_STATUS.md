## ⚠️ What Needs Configuration (5 minutes)

### **Contact Form** - Needs EmailJS Setup

**Current Status:** Form works, but needs EmailJS credentials to send emails.

**What to do:**
1. Sign up at [emailjs.com](https://www.emailjs.com/) (free, 2 minutes)
2. Get your 3 keys (2 minutes)
3. Update 2 files (1 minute):
   - `contact.html` line 16: Replace `YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY`
   - `js/main.js` lines 125-126: Replace `YOUR_SERVICE_ID` and `YOUR_TEMPLATE_ID`

**OR** use the backend:
- Fill in `.env` file with your email credentials
- Run `npm start`
- Form will work on `http://localhost:3000`
