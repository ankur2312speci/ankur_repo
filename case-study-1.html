The Challenge

Our client, a mid-size e-commerce brand, was struggling with low organic visibility and declining traffic. Despite having quality products, they were losing market share to competitors who ranked higher in search results. Their website had technical SEO issues, thin content, and poor keyword targeting.

Our Strategy

We developed a comprehensive SEO strategy focusing on three key areas:

  • Technical SEO: Fixed site speed, mobile responsiveness, and crawlability issues
  • Content Optimization: Created high-quality, keyword-rich product descriptions and blog content
  • Link Building: Built authoritative backlinks through strategic partnerships and content marketing

Implementation

Over 6 months, we executed a phased approach:

Month 1-2

Technical Audit & Fixes

Comprehensive site audit, fixing 200+ technical issues, improving page speed by 60%

Month 3-4

Content Strategy

Optimized 500+ product pages, created 50+ blog posts, improved keyword targeting

Month 5-6

Link Building & Optimization

Acquired 150+ quality backlinks, ongoing optimization based on performance data
