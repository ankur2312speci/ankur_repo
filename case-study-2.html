The Challenge

A fast-growing SaaS company was struggling with high customer acquisition costs and inefficient ad spend. Their PPC campaigns were generating leads, but the cost per acquisition was unsustainable for their business model. They needed to scale their marketing efforts while maintaining profitability.

Our Strategy

We implemented a data-driven PPC strategy with three core components:

  • Audience Segmentation: Created highly targeted audience segments based on behavior and intent signals
  • Bid Optimization: Implemented automated bidding strategies using machine learning
  • Landing Page Optimization: Developed conversion-focused landing pages with A/B testing

Implementation

Over 8 months, we executed a comprehensive PPC transformation:

Month 1-2

Campaign Restructure

Reorganized campaigns by product lines, implemented conversion tracking, and set up automated bidding

Month 3-5

Optimization Phase

Continuous A/B testing, keyword refinement, and audience expansion based on performance data

Month 6-8

Scaling & Growth

Increased ad spend 3x while maintaining target CPA, expanded to new channels and markets
