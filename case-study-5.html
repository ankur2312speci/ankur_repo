The Challenge

A local restaurant chain was invisible in local search results, losing customers to competitors who appeared in "near me" searches. They needed to dominate local SEO to drive foot traffic and increase bookings.

Our Strategy

  • Google Business Profile: Optimized all location profiles with complete information
  • Local Citations: Built consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across directories
  • Local Content: Created location-specific landing pages and content
  • Review Management: Implemented review generation and response strategy
