Email Marketing Best Practices for 2024

February 20, 2024 By David Kim 6 min read

Email marketing remains one of the most effective marketing channels, with an impressive ROI. However, to achieve success, you need to follow current best practices. Here are the email marketing strategies that will help you improve open rates and conversions in 2024.

1. Personalization and Segmentation

Generic emails don't cut it anymore. Use personalization tokens and segment your email list based on demographics, behavior, and preferences. Personalized emails have higher open and click-through rates.

2. Mobile Optimization

Over 60% of emails are opened on mobile devices. Ensure your emails are fully responsive and optimized for mobile viewing. Use a mobile-friendly design with clear CTAs and readable fonts.

3. Subject Line Optimization

Your subject line is the first impression. Keep it concise (under 50 characters), create urgency, and avoid spam triggers. A/B test different subject lines to find what works best for your audience.

4. Automation and Workflows

Implement email automation workflows for welcome series, abandoned cart recovery, and lead nurturing. Automated emails generate more engagement and help you stay connected with your audience.

5. Value-First Content

Focus on providing value rather than just promoting products. Share helpful tips, industry insights, and exclusive content. This builds trust and keeps subscribers engaged.

6. Compliance and Deliverability

Ensure compliance with GDPR, CAN-SPAM, and other regulations. Use double opt-in, provide clear unsubscribe options, and maintain good sender reputation to improve deliverability rates.
